Run it on your own feeds

Optical vs chip, automated stats vs human operators, incumbent vendor vs challenger. It runs in Docker on your machine; nothing leaves your building.

01Export two feeds

Any two sources of the same games, in metres from centre ice or the centre spot, with each provider's own quality flag.

02Run the audit

Orientation and clock offsets are fitted from the data. No calibration session, no manual labelling.

03Gate and review

Per game and per venue: pass or fail against your limit, with confidence intervals and a review queue linked to video.

docker build -t trackgate .
docker run --rm -v "$PWD/feeds:/data" trackgate compare \
  --continuous /data/optical.jsonl.bz2 --snapshot-events /data/events.json \
  --snapshot-frames /data/frames.json --home "Home Team" --out /data/report.json

How to read this

  • Neither provider is ground truth. When the two are metres apart, at least one of them is metres off; the review queue exists to settle which.
  • Every event is shifted within ±1 s to the moment the sources agree best, so the gaps shown are not clock artefacts.
  • StatsBomb 360 only records players visible in the broadcast frame, so "PFF: estimated" rows are players who were on screen while PFF estimated their position.
  • Data: StatsBomb open data (events, 360 freeze frames) and the PFF FC 2022 World Cup dataset (broadcast tracking). Data provided by StatsBomb.
