    Runs inside your network

    TrackGate ships as a container. Feeds stay on your machines; only the report leaves, if you send it.

    docker run --rm -v "$PWD/feeds:/data" trackgate compare \
  --continuous /data/optical.jsonl.bz2 \
  --snapshot-events /data/events.json \
  --snapshot-frames /data/frames.json \
  --home "Home Team" --out /data/report.json
    Pilot on your data:vahit.feryat@gmail.com

    How to read this run

    • Neither provider is ground truth. When the two are metres apart, at least one of them is metres off; the review queue exists to settle which.
    • Every event is shifted within ±1 s to the moment the sources agree best, so the gaps are not clock artefacts.
    • StatsBomb 360 records only players visible in the broadcast frame, so "PFF estimated" positions are players who were on screen while PFF estimated them.
    • Data: StatsBomb open data and the PFF FC 2022 World Cup dataset. Data provided by StatsBomb.
